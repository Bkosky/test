# Live UK used-car listings, £10,000–£15,000, reliable makes (newest registration, lowest mileage)

**Date checked: 20 October 2026.** Listings change daily, and cars sell or are repriced within days. Treat every listing below as a snapshot. Re-check the live URL before acting.

**How the data was gathered (read this first):**
- Every attempt to fetch a listing site directly was blocked. The session's egress proxy returned HTTP 403 "connect_rejected" for motorpoint.co.uk, cinch.co.uk, theaa.com, arnoldclark.com, autotrader.co.uk, cazoo.co.uk, carwow.co.uk, cargurus.co.uk and autouncle.co.uk.
- All listing data below therefore comes from **web-search result snippets and summaries** of the listing pages. These were not opened or confirmed by hand.
- The search index does not show when each page was cached. I could **not confirm that any single listing is still live on 20 Oct 2026**. Several snippets say "Coming Soon" or "on sale within 7–10 days", which means they were captured before the car went on sale.
- "Confirmed" below means the figure appeared in a search snippet tied to that URL. "Inferred" means I worked it out (for example, a stock number tied to a URL pattern).
- One date hint: a Kia approved-used listing ID contains "20260808" (Aug 2026). That suggests at least some indexed pages are recent. Motorpoint stock numbers in the 1.61m–1.69m range also look recent compared with older 1.48m–1.50m IDs.

## What are the newest/lowest-mileage examples available at ~£10k, ~£12.5k and ~£15k?

### Takeaway
At about £14–15k, the best age/mileage buys are 2025-plate city cars and superminis with delivery or low mileage: Hyundai i10 (1,949 mi, £14,549), Suzuki Swift (3,810 mi, £14,449), Dacia Sandero Journey (~3,100 mi, £14,849) and Hyundai i20 (7,538 mi, £14,549), all at Motorpoint. At about £12.5k, the newest cars are 2024 Toyota Aygo X (£12,499–£13,949, 10–27k mi) and a 2024 Kia Picanto with 3,641 mi at £13,498 (Arnold Clark). At about £10k, you get roughly 2022–2023 city cars (Picanto, Sandero). Toyota Yaris Hybrid and Honda Jazz Hybrid are a year or more older at this money: 2023 Yaris for about £14.5–15k at 12–38k miles, and 2023 Jazz from £16.5k, which is over budget.

## Which sellers (manufacturer approved-used schemes, big supermarkets) offer the best price-to-age/mileage, warranty and return policies?

### Takeaway
Motorpoint dominates the low-mileage 2024–2025 stock in this band. It advertises prices benchmarked daily, a 14-day money-back guarantee and a free 3-month warranty where the manufacturer warranty has lapsed (most cars here still carry the maker's warranty). Cinch also offers 14-day returns and a 3-month warranty. Manufacturer schemes trade a slightly higher price for longer cover: Kia Assured (balance of the 7-yr warranty, topped up to at least 12 months) and Toyota Relax (12 months/10,000 miles of cover renewed at each Toyota dealer service, up to 10 years/100k miles).

## Do prices look in line with market value (flag any suspiciously cheap listings as potential scams)?

### Takeaway
The main-retailer prices found sit within the market ranges published by aggregators. None of the listings compiled looks suspiciously cheap. The cheapest-for-year cars (e.g. 2023 Yaris at £12k) are explained by high mileage (60k+). One aggregator data point (2025 Sandero Stepway from about £5,400 on CarGurus) is anomalously low and should be treated with caution (possible write-off category, data error or scam). UK scam warnings centre on prices around half of market value, deposit requests before viewing, and cloned dealer identities.

### Cited Findings

Motorpoint's 2024 Fabias at £13,899–£15,999 sit at the low-to-mid end — [AutoUncle Fabia 2024](https://www.autouncle.co.uk/en-gb/used-cars/Skoda/Fabia/y-2024); [Motorpoint Fabia](https://www.motorpoint.co.uk/used-cars/skoda/fabia)

- CarGurus (South East): 2025 Sandero Stepway averages about £15,787 across 23 listings, ranging from about £5,400 to £17,900. **The ~£5,400 low end is anomalous** for a 2025 car — [CarGurus Sandero Stepway 2025 SE](https://www.cargurus.co.uk/Cars/l-Used-2025-Dacia-Sandero-Stepway-South-East-England-c65624_L948712)

### Inferences
- Rough fair-value benchmarks for Oct 2026 from the compiled data: 2025 i10/Swift/Sandero/i20 at about £13.8–15.5k; 2024 Aygo X at about £12.5–14k; 2024 Fabia/Polo at about £13.9–15k; 2023 Yaris Hybrid at about £14.5–16k (lower only with high mileage).
- Any 2024–2025 car from these makes advertised well below about £9–10k (e.g. a 2025 Sandero at £5–6k, or a 2023 Yaris Hybrid with low miles under £11k) should be treated as a write-off (Cat S/N), a clocked or financed car, or a scam until an HPI/MOT check and an in-person viewing say otherwise.
- Large retailers (Motorpoint, Arnold Clark, cinch) and franchised dealers carry very low scam risk. The risk sits mainly with private and Marketplace-style listings and cloned "independent dealer" ads.

- Autotrader's own safety guidance and price indicator ("Great/Good/Fair price") could not be retrieved.
- I could not check the anomalous CarGurus £5.4k Sandero Stepway figure for write-off category or verify it.

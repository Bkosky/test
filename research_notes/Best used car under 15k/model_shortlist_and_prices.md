# Best used car for £10k–£15k (UK, October 2026): which models give the newest year and lowest mileage

Method note: most figures are dealer and marketplace **asking prices** (not sold prices). They were gathered on 20 Oct 2026 from search-result snapshots of Autotrader, AutoUncle, cinch, PistonHeads, Arnold Clark, Stoneacre, Lookers, RAC Cars, Listers, CarGurus and eBay. WebFetch could not resolve most listing domains (DNS failure), so I could not open the full pages. Figures come from search snippets and should be treated as indicative. Where a source gave its own data date, that date is noted.

## Q1. What year and mileage does £10k / £12.5k / £15k buy for each candidate model?

### Takeaway
At £15k the newest cars are 2024 superminis with fewer than 15k miles: Hyundai i10/i20, Skoda Fabia, Seat Ibiza, MG3 Hybrid+, Kia Picanto, Renault Clio and Dacia Sandero. The same money buys 2023 cars with about 20–45k miles in a Toyota Yaris Hybrid, Kia Ceed, VW Polo or Honda Jazz (the Jazz usually costs more). At £12.5k, 2024 cars are still possible in the MG3, i10, Fabia, Clio and Sandero. At £10k only the 2023–24 Sandero and Clio have listings starting near that price; for the other models I found no 2023+ listings at £10k.

## Q2. Which models depreciate fastest (giving the newest or lowest-mileage car for the money) without being unreliable?

### Takeaway
Small EVs (Leaf, Corsa Electric, Fiat 500e, Zoe) depreciate fastest, losing about 70% in three years, but small EVs are among the least reliable small cars in the 2025 survey. Among petrol and hybrid superminis, the MG3 Hybrid+ and the Hyundai i10/i20 lose value fastest, so they give the most car for the money. The Hyundai i10 is also the most reliable small car in What Car?'s 2025 survey. Toyota and Honda hybrids depreciate slowly.

### Cited Findings
- **Fastest depreciators in 2026:** the Nissan Leaf loses about 73% in three years (£29,000 new, £7,800 at three years) and the Vauxhall Corsa Electric about 69% (£33,000 new, £10,100). The Mazda MX-30 and Fiat 500e are also around 69–73%. The petrol Fiat 500C loses about 34% — [Exchangemycar fastest depreciating cars 2026](https://www.exchangemycar.co.uk/blog/fastest-depreciating-cars-uk/)
- Carsa says the fastest-depreciating cars are often the best used buys, and that values on capable, well-specified models are stabilising after the 2022–24 correction — [Carsa: what is car depreciation 2026](https://www.carsa.co.uk/blog/what-is-car-depreciation)
- **What Car? 2025 Reliability Survey (32,000+ UK owners), small cars:** the Hyundai i10 scored 100%, one of only four models in the whole survey to do so. The Toyota Aygo X (2022+) scored 99.7% and the Mini Hatch (2014–24) 99.1%. The lowest small-car scores were the Renault Zoe (85.1%, 39% fault rate, mostly battery), Vauxhall Corsa Electric (87.6%) and MG3 (87.9%) — [Autocar most reliable cars 2025](https://www.autocar.co.uk/car-news/slideshow/most-reliable-cars-2025-%E2%80%93-and-least); [What Car? most reliable cars](https://www.whatcar.com/news/most-reliable-cars/n27337)
- The MG3 Hybrid+ new RRP is £17,995+, and 2024 cars with 3–21k miles are listed at £12,490–£14,495 — [Autotrader MG3 2024](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/cars/used/mg/mg3/2024); [Stoneacre MG3 Hybrid+](https://www.stoneacre.co.uk/used-cars/mg/3-hybridplus)
- Used i20 values are down 11.8% year on year (CarGurus, all years) — [CarGurus i20](https://www.cargurus.co.uk/Cars/l-Used-Hyundai-i20-d3237)
- The 2023 Yaris Hybrid averages £17,662 used, with a median of 20,600 miles (AutoUncle, August 2026). That is a strong price for a three-year-old supermini — [AutoUncle Yaris Hybrid 2023](https://www.autouncle.co.uk/en-gb/used-cars/Toyota/Yaris%20Hybrid/y-2023)

### Inferences
- The MG3 Hybrid+ loses roughly 20–30% in 1–2 years (£17,995+ new against about £12.5k–£14.5k for a 2024 car). That makes it the best value for newness and mileage, and its 7-year warranty offsets the weaker 87.9% reliability score. Note that the survey figure may reflect the previous petrol MG3 (pre-2024); see Gaps.
- The Hyundai i10 and i20 combine fast depreciation with top-tier reliability (i10 100%) and a transferable 5-year unlimited-mileage warranty. **They are the strongest match to the buyer's priorities.**
- A used small EV such as the Corsa Electric gives the newest car for the money because of very steep depreciation, but its weak reliability scores and battery concerns go against the buyer's reliability priority.

### Gaps
- The survey category for the MG3 is not clear: the 87.9% may cover the pre-2024 petrol MG3 rather than the 2024+ Hybrid+.
- I found no 2026 percentage-depreciation figures for specific petrol superminis such as the Polo, Fabia and Yaris.

## Q3. Which models still carry transferable manufacturer warranty at that age?

### Takeaway
Kia (7yr/100k), MG (7yr/80k) and Hyundai (5yr unlimited miles) warranties transfer to the next owner, so a 2023–24 car still has 4–5+ years of cover. Toyota's long cover comes from its "Relax" scheme (the user's "Relentless" is a misnomer). Relax adds a year of cover with each Toyota-dealer service, up to 10 years, so a used Yaris can be brought back under cover. VW, Skoda, Seat, Renault, Dacia and Honda have shorter standard warranties, which will be expired or nearly expired on a 2023 car.

### Cited Findings
- **Kia:** 7 years / 100,000 miles, unlimited mileage in years 1–3, and transferable to later owners — [Lloyd Motor Group Kia Ceed](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/usedcars/kia/ceed); [Motorpoint used cars with long warranties](https://www.motorpoint.co.uk/best/used-cars-with-long-warranties)
- One source states Kia is the only brand among these that does not require servicing conditions to reach the full term — [Carwow best new car warranties 2026](https://www.carwow.co.uk/best/cars-with-good-warranties-0170)
- **Kia approved used:** "Kia Approved" cars under 20 months old and under 20,000 miles get a full new 7-year warranty from the day you drive away. "Kia Assured" cars carry the balance of the original 7 years, with an option to extend — [Lloyd Motor Group Kia Ceed](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/usedcars/kia/ceed)
- **MG:** 7 years / 80,000 miles and transferable. Wear items (except tyres) are covered for the first 12 months and paintwork for 3 years — [Carwow best new car warranties 2026](https://www.carwow.co.uk/best/cars-with-good-warranties-0170); [Nationwide Vehicle Contracts warranty guide](https://www.nationwidevehiclecontracts.co.uk/guides/ask-nvc/car-manufacturer-warranty-guide)
- **Hyundai:** 5 years with unlimited mileage for private buyers, and fully transferable — [Carwow best new car warranties 2026](https://www.carwow.co.uk/best/cars-with-good-warranties-0170)
- **Toyota Relax:** the base warranty is 3 years / 60,000 miles. Each service at an authorised Toyota dealer adds another year (or 10,000 miles), up to 10 years from first registration. One source says the hybrid battery can be extended to 15 years via a Hybrid Health Check; this is not confirmed with Toyota — [Warranty24 manufacturer warranty comparison 2026](https://www.warranty24.co.uk/manufacturer-warranty-comparison/); [Auto Express warranty advice](https://www.autoexpress.co.uk/tips-advice/103763/car-warranty-advice-manufacturer-warranty-cover-and-extended-warranties)
- Approved-used schemes at this budget typically include a 12-month warranty — [Honest John best used cars under £15,000](https://www.honestjohn.co.uk/guides/best-used-cars-under-15-000/)
- Independent dealers may offer only short cover; one 2023 Ceed listing came with a "free 1 year warranty" — [Carsa 2023 Ceed](https://www.carsa.co.uk/vehicles/used/lb23vop)

### Inferences (remaining cover, counted from first registration)
- 2024 MG3 Hybrid+: covered until about 2031 or 80k miles.
- 2023 Kia Ceed or 2024 Picanto: covered until about 2030–31 or 100k miles.
- 2024 Hyundai i10/i20: covered until about 2029, unlimited miles.
- 2023 Yaris Hybrid: the base 3-year cover ends in 2026, but Relax can extend it a year at a time if the car is serviced at a Toyota dealer.
- 2023–24 Polo, Fabia, Ibiza, Clio, Sandero and Jazz: assuming the usual 3-year/60k cover (not confirmed per brand in this research), 2023 cars are at or near expiry and 2024 cars have about a year left. A dealer or extended warranty would be needed.

### Gaps
- Current 2026 standard warranty terms for VW, Skoda, Seat, Renault, Dacia, Honda and Ford were not confirmed. (Renault and Dacia may have introduced dealer-service-linked extensions; this needs checking.)
- Whether the 5-year Hyundai cover is fully transferable to private second owners in the UK was confirmed by only one source. The US results I found were not relevant.

### Inferences
- A pre-registered or delivery-mileage MG3 Hybrid+ at about £14k (2026 plate, almost 7 years of warranty, about 60mpg-class hybrid) is the newest car this budget can buy. The trade-off is MG's weaker reliability-survey record.
- For a cash buyer who also wants top reliability, a nearly new 2024 Hyundai i10 or i20 with under 10k miles at about £14k–£15.5k (warranty to about 2029) is the stronger balance.
- A brand-new Dacia Sandero (from £14,730) or a new Hyundai i10 may compete with nearly new used cars. The buyer should compare new cash prices from a broker such as Carwow before buying used.
